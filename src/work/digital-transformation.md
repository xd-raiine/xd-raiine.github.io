## Overview

We worked with an edtech non-profit on a program designed to help small businesses integrate technology into their day-to-day operations. The goal was to equip them with tools for growth. Not through surface-level trends, but through sustainable, behind-the-scenes transformation.

## Challenge

The original materials leaned heavily into corporate aesthetics, which felt intimidating and impersonal to independent business owners. We needed to soften the tone while still making the program feel forward-thinking. The solution: blend retro-inspired visuals with a clean, modern edge to create something both approachable and distinct.

## Approach

Inspired by the visual language of Asia’s economic boom era, we crafted a look that nodded to progress while staying grounded in familiarity. The messaging focused on empowerment, reminding small businesses that transformation is possible and accessible, and that tech integration isn’t just for big corporations.

## Outcome

The refreshed identity helped the program stand out from other initiatives while feeling more relatable to the target audience. It offered a clear, welcoming path to digital transformation: one that resonated with independent business owners and fit the program’s mission of practical, accessible growth.
