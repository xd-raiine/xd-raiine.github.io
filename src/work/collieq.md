## Overview

Our team built CollieQ, a queue management platform for small businesses trying to stay afloat during the pandemic. It was part of a digital marketing bootcamp, and the goal was to solve a real problem with a simple, scalable solution. We focused on essential businesses struggling with foot traffic, long lines and public health concerns, and gave them a way to manage crowds without losing their minds.

## Challenge

Most existing solutions were clunky, expensive, or aimed at giant retailers. Meanwhile, small shops were left taping signs to windows and hoping people followed the rules. We saw a gap: help smaller businesses manage traffic better, enforce distancing, and create safer spaces without a full-time bouncer at the door.

## Approach

We explored a few directions, like transit tracking and appointment scheduling. After a lot of team discussion, we landed on queue management as the most viable path. We built out a working prototype, complete with UX flows, visual identity and a three-tier pricing model. Our value prop was simple: reduce wait times, improve safety, and give business owners better data to work with. The brand leaned on clarity, ease of use and approachability.

## Outcome

This wasn’t a real product. It was a bootcamp project, but it easily beat the competition. Instructors praised the professionalism of our pitch, the strong, cohesive brand and the depth of our strategy. They were so impressed they hired most of our team on as interns. We walked away with hands-on experience building and pitching a full product, from research to rollout.
