## Overview

We partnered with a seasoned restaurateur launching a new fusion food franchise in the heart of the financial district. The goal: serve bold, fast, flavourful meals for working professionals on a tight lunch break, and make sure the brand grabs attention fast.

## Challenge

The brand needed to stand out in a space dominated by soft greens and “clean eating” aesthetics. We focused on younger professionals and the media they already engaged with, then compared competitors to find a bold, untapped visual lane: something fast, edgy and unmissable.

## Approach

We took cues from action video games and hyper-energetic digital media. The brand used aggressive type, punchy copy and in-your-face visuals designed to stop foot traffic and make a statement. It was unapologetic, loud, and totally unlike anything else in the neighbourhood.

## Outcome

The brand launch generated buzz and immediate foot traffic. It led to a gym partnership and repeat business from the client, who came back for more, including murals, signage and print collateral. The identity helped set the tone for a growing franchise with a bold attitude.
